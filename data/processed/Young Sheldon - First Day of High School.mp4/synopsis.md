# Sheldon Goes To High School

## Summary
Sheldon Cooper is driven to high school by his mother, Mary Cooper, who attempts to play a driving game with him and discusses his intelligence and potential intimidation by peers. Mary expresses anxiety about his first day, fearing he might be bullied. Upon arrival, Mary tries to convince Sheldon to remove his bow tie, even offering a trip to Radio Shack, but Sheldon suggests he might start a fad. In the high school hallway, Mary advises Sheldon to tell anyone who bothers him that his dad is a football coach and his brother is a football player. Sheldon then loudly congratulates a pregnant girl. Finally, Mary escorts Sheldon to his homeroom, bids him goodbye, and questions if he is crying, attributing it to allergies or the smell of ammonia. Sheldon then enters his classroom.
